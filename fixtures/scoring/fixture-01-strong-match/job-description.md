## About Northgate Fleet Systems

Northgate Fleet Systems builds dispatch, routing, and fleet-visibility software for regional delivery and trucking companies across North America. Our platform helps operators assign jobs, track vehicles in real time, and keep customers informed from pickup to delivery. We're a product company, not an agency — every engineer owns a real piece of the system end to end.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Senior Software Engineer to join our Dispatch Platform team (6 engineers, roughly even split between backend and frontend). You'll help us finish migrating our dispatch and billing services off a legacy monolith and onto event-driven microservices, and you'll own real-time features on the fleet-tracking dashboard our dispatchers use every day. This role carries a standard on-call rotation once ramped up — you'll be expected to operate what you build, not just ship it.

### Responsibilities
- Design and build backend services in Java/Spring Boot for dispatch, billing, and fleet-tracking domains, with an emphasis on deployment independence and fault isolation between services
- Build and maintain real-time dashboard features in Angular (NgRx for state management) consumed by dispatchers throughout their shift
- Design and consume event streams (Kafka) for vehicle-location updates and job-status changes across services
- Own data modeling and query performance for PostgreSQL-backed services, including schema design and index tuning under production load
- Implement and maintain authentication/authorization (OAuth2/JWT) across customer- and dispatcher-facing APIs
- Participate in a standard on-call rotation for the services you own
- Contribute to CI/CD pipeline health (GitHub Actions, Docker) and help onboard new engineers onto the team's tooling

### Requirements
- 4+ years of professional backend software engineering experience
- Strong hands-on experience with Java and Spring Boot in production
- Experience building and maintaining frontend applications in Angular, including state management (NgRx or similar)
- Working knowledge of event-driven architectures and message/event streaming (Kafka or equivalent)
- Solid PostgreSQL or other relational database experience, including query optimization
- Experience with OAuth2/JWT-based authentication and role-based access control
- Comfort with Docker and CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions or equivalent)
- Experience testing services and UIs (JUnit, Jest, or equivalent frameworks)

### Nice to Have
- Experience with AWS (ECS, RDS, CloudWatch) or a comparable cloud platform
- Exposure to infrastructure-as-code tooling (Terraform or equivalent)
- Experience with Redis or another caching layer
- Prior experience in logistics, fleet management, or a similarly operational domain
