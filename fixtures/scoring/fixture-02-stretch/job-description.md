Everline Financial Technologies builds the payments and fraud-detection infrastructure behind a network of mid-market e-commerce platforms. Our Payments team owns transaction processing, ledger integrity, and real-time fraud scoring for every transaction that flows through the network.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Staff Backend Engineer to help lead our Payments & Fraud team (8 engineers). You'll own core transaction-processing services and set technical direction for how we evolve our fraud-detection and compliance posture as transaction volume grows. This is a deeply regulated domain — correctness and auditability matter as much as throughput.

### Responsibilities
- Design and evolve backend services (Java/Spring Boot) for transaction processing, ledger integrity, and settlement

- Own PCI-DSS compliance requirements across the services you build, including audit-logging and data-handling standards

- Partner with our Risk and Compliance teams to translate regulatory requirements into engineering constraints
- Mentor engineers across the team on distributed-systems correctness and financial-domain modeling
- Participate in a standard on-call rotation for payment-critical services
