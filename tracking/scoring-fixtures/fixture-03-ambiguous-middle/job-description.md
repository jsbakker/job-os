## About Bellwood Cloud Systems

Bellwood Cloud Systems builds observability and infrastructure-monitoring tools for engineering teams at mid-market SaaS companies. Our own Developer Infrastructure team builds the internal platform that every other engineering team at Bellwood ships on top of.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Senior or Staff Platform Engineer (banded by experience) to help lead our internal developer platform: the shared Kubernetes infrastructure, service mesh, and deployment tooling that every product team at Bellwood depends on. You'll set technical direction for how services get built, deployed, and operated company-wide, and you'll be a go-to resource for teams adopting the platform.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate our internal Kubernetes platform, including cluster architecture, upgrades, and multi-tenant workload isolation
- Own our service mesh (Istio) configuration for traffic management, mutual TLS, and cross-service observability
- Write and maintain platform services and tooling primarily in Go
- Design and evolve internal developer tooling and paved-path templates adopted by multiple product teams
- Own cloud infrastructure and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform) for the platform's AWS footprint
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines used company-wide, and drive adoption of engineering best practices across teams
- Instrument platform and product services for observability, working closely with teams to improve monitoring coverage

### Requirements
- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, including infrastructure or platform-focused work (Staff-level candidates typically have 6+)
- Production experience with Go for backend/platform services
- Hands-on experience operating Kubernetes in production, including cluster administration
- Experience with a major cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and infrastructure-as-code tooling (Terraform or equivalent)
- Experience designing CI/CD pipelines and driving their adoption across multiple teams
- Track record of building internal tools or platforms used by other engineering teams

### Nice to Have
- Experience with service mesh technologies (Istio, Linkerd, or similar)
- Experience with observability/monitoring tooling and instrumentation
- Experience leading or contributing to a service migration from a monolith to a distributed architecture
- Background at a company building developer or infrastructure tooling as its core product
