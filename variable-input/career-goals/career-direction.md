# Career Direction
I want to keep growing as a backend-leaning full-stack engineer, with more ownership over system design and infrastructure decisions rather than just implementing what's handed to me. The event-driven work I'm ramping up on now (Kafka, AWS infra) is genuinely new territory for me and I want to lean into it over the next couple of years.

I'm not trying to become a DevOps or SRE specialist — I like writing application code too much to give that up — but I want enough infra fluency to make good architectural calls and not be dependent on someone else for every deployment or scaling decision.

For now I'd rather go deeper as an individual contributor (Staff-track) than move into people management, though I'm not ruling that out long-term.
